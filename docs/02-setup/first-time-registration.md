# Setting yourself up

A one-time setup, usually five minutes. You'll sign in to Krista, connect your
calendar so she knows which meetings to join, and check that your meetings are
showing.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- **Your Krista sign-in** — the email and password your administrator set up for
  you. If your welcome email hasn't arrived, they can resend it.
- **Your work Microsoft account** — the organizational email and password you
  normally use, along with whatever multi-factor method you usually approve with.
- **Your Krista address** — the web address your organization uses, something
  along the lines of `us.krista.app`. Worth bookmarking; you'll use it daily.

## Step by step

### 1. Sign in to Krista

Open your Krista address in a browser and sign in with your email and password.
There's a **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page if you need it.

*If sign-in gives you an error*, your administrator may need to unblock your
Microsoft account — worth asking them rather than retrying.

### 2. Open the agent

From the home screen, open the agent in your list. Depending on how your
organization has set things up, it may be labelled **New Conversation Agent**
rather than "Meeting Agent" — the two names refer to the same thing, so don't
worry if the wording doesn't match. Your administrator can point you at the right
one if there's more than one in your list.

### 3. Complete your user setup

You'll land on a screen inviting you to **Complete Your User Setup to Unlock Full
Access**. Click the button to carry on.

### 4. Connect your calendar

Krista opens a **Connect My Calendar** conversation and lets you know she needs to
send you to a sign-in page. Click **Send**, then sign in with your organizational
Microsoft account and approve the usual multi-factor prompt.

This is the step that matters most — Krista works from your calendar, so until
it's connected she has no way of knowing which meetings to join.

*If you're told your email address isn't on the registered domains list*, your
Krista account was probably created with an address outside your organization's
domain. Your administrator can confirm which email to use.

### 5. Check it completed

Once sign-in is approved, the **Connect My Calendar** conversation shows as
**Completed**, with a message confirming you're set up. You can close that tab.

### 6. Close the agent fully, then reopen it

**This part is easy to miss:** close the agent tab completely and open it again
from your agent list. Refreshing the page isn't enough.

Once it's reopened, click the **Home** button — the house icon on the left — and
your upcoming meetings should appear. If they don't, **Refresh Events** usually
sorts it.

Calendars can take a few minutes to sync after connecting, so if nothing shows
straight away it's worth giving it a moment before assuming something's wrong.

### 7. Have a look at the help

On the home screen, the **?** icon in the top-right corner opens the built-in User
Guide, including short video tutorials covering the dashboard, meeting templates,
and how Krista takes part in your meetings. It's the quickest answer to most
questions, and worth five minutes now rather than hunting later.

## If something doesn't go to plan

**"Authentication Failed"** — this one looks alarming and usually isn't. It's a
known browser timing quirk rather than a real failure. Click **Refresh Events**
on your dashboard and it resolves.

**Your meetings aren't showing** — give the calendar a few minutes to sync, then
try **Refresh Events**. If they're still not there, reconnect your calendar:
**Conversation Agent → Initiate → Connect My Calendar**, and sign in again.

**A 403 error, a missing agent, or a permissions message** — these are account
matters rather than anything you can fix from your side. Your Krista
administrator can sort them out.

## What happens next

Once your calendar is connected, Krista starts appearing in the meetings that are
in scope for your team. [Getting the best from
Krista](../03-operating/meeting-hygiene.md) is a short read covering what to
expect and how to ask her to pause or stay off the record.
